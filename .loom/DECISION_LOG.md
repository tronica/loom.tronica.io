# Decision Log

**Purpose:** Record architectural decisions, rationale, and tradeoffs for future reference.

Each entry should include the decision, context, options considered, and the chosen path with reasoning. This helps maintain consistency and provides background for future maintainers.

---

## Format

```markdown
### [YYYY-MM-DD] Decision Title

**Context:** Why this decision was needed.

**Options Considered:**
- Option A
- Option B
- Option C

**Decision:** What was chosen.

**Rationale:** Why this option was selected.

**Consequences:** Expected impacts, tradeoffs, or follow-up work.

---
```
